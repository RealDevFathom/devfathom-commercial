# Agency glossary

Plain-language meanings of words used in the [agency guide](agency-guide.md), including terms shared across DevFathom.

> **Status: IMPLEMENTED (repository scope).** Checked against code at `6e425380`, the base of PR #58; see the guide's [repository evidence](agency-guide.md#repository-evidence). Availability depends on enabled modules and effective permissions. Items marked **TODO(confirm)** still need checking.

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Admin** | The role for people who run the organization: settings, invitations, roles, clients and Finance. |
| **API token** | A secret key that lets another system connect to DevFathom. Keep it private, like a password. |
| **Audit log** | A record of who did what, and when. |
| **Board** | A project's work laid out in columns of tasks. It can be imported from Trello. |
| **Channel** | A chat room for a team or topic. |
| **Client** | A business or person your agency does work for. |
| **Custom role** | A role your organization creates, with its own list of permissions. |
| **Dashboard** | The overview screen you see first. |
| **Employee** | The role for day-to-day staff. |
| **HR** | The role for people who handle leave, performance reviews and resignations. HR does not see Finance. |
| **Invitation** | An offer to join your organization in DevFathom, sent to a new user. |
| **Contract** | An agreement with a client, recorded on the client. An invoice can be created from it. |
| **Department** | The team a person belongs to, such as Engineering or Design. Admins manage the organization's department list. |
| **Invoice** | A bill you send to a client. |
| **Leave request** | An employee's request for time off. |
| **Module** | A part of DevFathom enabled for your organization. Agency-specific modules are Agency Core, Projects, Clients and Finance; shared features such as Chat, HR and Email have separate controls. |
| **Organization** | Your business inside DevFathom. Its data is kept separate from every other organization. |
| **Payment** | Money received against an invoice. |
| **Permission** | Permission to do one specific thing in DevFathom. |
| **Permission override** | A permission given to, or taken away from, one person only. |
| **Project** | A piece of work you do for a client. |
| **Resignation** | A record of a staff member leaving. |
| **Review** | A staff performance review, run in review cycles by HR or an Admin. Not a client review. |
| **SCIM** | A way to add and remove users automatically from your company's account system. TODO(confirm) which plans include it. |
| **SSO (single sign-on)** | Signing in with your company account instead of a separate password. TODO(confirm) which plans include it. |
| **Support session** | Temporary access that lets authorized DevFathom support staff act as an active Admin in your organization. It requires a reason, notifies active Admins, and is recorded in the platform audit log. |
| **Task** | One piece of work on a board, usually given to one person. |
| **Team statistics** | Figures about each person's work, such as open, overdue and finished tasks, meetings and notes. The team-wide view is for Admins. |
| **Time entry** | A record of hours spent on work, typed in by hand. |
| **Two-step sign-in (2FA)** | Signing in with your password plus a code from an authenticator app. Each person turns it on for themselves. |
| **Webhook** | An automatic message DevFathom sends to another system when something happens. |
