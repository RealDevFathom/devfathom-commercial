# Glossary

_Short meanings of words you will see in DevFathom and in these guides. Listed A to Z._

**Admin**
A staff member with wide control over their business's settings and staff. Being an admin does **not** automatically allow someone to dispense medicines, work on the controlled-drugs register (safety) or approve regulated medicine list changes. See _Protected job_.

**Audit log / activity log**
A record of who did what in the system, and when.

**Barcode**
The striped code on a pack. Scanning it at the counter finds the right medicine and pack size.

**Batch (lot)**
A group of packs made at the same time, usually with the same expiry date. DevFathom tracks stock by batch.

**Branch**
One shop location of your business. A business can have several branches, and staff can be limited to the branches they work in.

**Business (organisation)**
Your company as it appears in DevFathom. Each business sees only its own information.

**Cash drawer**
The till at the counter. DevFathom keeps track of the cash going in and out of it during the day.

**Catalogue (medicine list)**
Your list of medicines, with each medicine's ingredients, form, pack sizes, MRP and barcodes.

**Catalogue review**
Confirming or rejecting regulated medicine-list changes proposed by connected sources before they go live. This is a _protected job_.

**Controlled-drugs register**
A record that the law requires for certain medicines. Working on it is part of the _safety_ protected job. **DRAFT – pending legal review:** TODO(confirm) the exact legal duties, pending pharmacist or legal review.

**Cutover**
The day you stop using your old system and use only DevFathom.

**Daily close (end-of-day close)**
Checking and closing the day's sales and cash at the end of a shift or day.

**Dispensing**
Handing over medicines against a prescription after it has been checked. This is a _protected job_.

**DRAP**
The Drug Regulatory Authority of Pakistan. It is the government body that regulates medicines, including recalls. **DRAFT – pending legal review.**

**GRN (goods received note)**
A record of what a supplier actually delivered, checked against what you ordered.

**Ingredient**
The active substance in a medicine.

**Khata**
A customer credit account. The customer takes goods now and pays later, and the system keeps the running balance.

**Loyalty**
Rewards for regular customers.

**Module / package**
A group of features. A business turns on the modules or packages it needs. TODO(confirm): which packages exist.

**MRP (maximum retail price)**
The retail price printed on a medicine's pack.

**Offline mode**
Letting some counter devices keep working when the internet is down. TODO(confirm): what works offline.

**Opening stock**
The stock you already have on your shelves when you start using DevFathom.

**Pack size (pack level)**
How a medicine is sold, for example a single strip, a box of strips, or a carton.

**Parallel run**
A period when you use your old system and DevFathom side by side, to check that they match before cutover.

**Permission**
Permission to do a particular thing in the system. Owners can give or remove permissions for each staff member.

**Pharmacist queue**
The list of prescriptions waiting for a pharmacist to check them.

**Presentation (form)**
The shape a medicine comes in, such as a tablet, syrup or injection.

**Prescriber**
The doctor or other professional who wrote a prescription.

**Prescription**
A written order from a prescriber for a patient's medicines.

**Protected job**
One of three pharmacy jobs that must each be given to a named person on purpose: _dispensing_, _safety_ and _catalogue review_. Admin rights and "all permissions" do not include them; they come only from a role or grant that names them. See the [pharmacy guide](pharmacy-guide.md#the-three-protected-jobs).

**Purchase order (PO)**
A formal order you send to a supplier.

**Quote**
A supplier's price offer before you order.

**Recall**
A notice that a medicine or batch must be taken off sale. **DRAFT – pending legal review.**

**Refund**
Giving a customer money back for a returned item.

**Requisition**
An internal request saying "we need to buy this", made before a purchase order.

**Role**
A set of permissions you give to a staff member. There are basic account roles (admin, employee, HR) and ready-made pharmacy roles such as Owner, Pharmacist, Manager and Cashier.

**Safety**
The protected job that covers the controlled-drugs register: viewing it, recording corrections, approving register incidents, preparing inspection packs and setting record-keeping holds. It is not a clinical check of the patient. This is a _protected job_.

**Stock count**
Counting what is actually on the shelves and comparing it to what the system says.

**Stock transfer**
Moving stock from one branch to another.

**Supplier credit**
Money a supplier owes you, for example after you return goods.

**Tender**
How a customer pays, for example cash or card. One sale can be paid with more than one tender.
