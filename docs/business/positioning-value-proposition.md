# Positioning and value proposition

## Evidence-backed position

DevFathom is a governed, multi-tenant operating workspace. Restaurant is its deepest vertical: menu-to-close operations sit beside inventory, purchasing, SOP execution, customer/loyalty, and insight workflows. Seller operators manage organization lifecycle, packages, entitlements, contracts, support access, and audit from a separate control plane.

- **IMPLEMENTED** — Restaurant M1–M8 capability evidence: [`docs/product/verticals/restaurant/PRD.md`](../product/verticals/restaurant/PRD.md), [`docs/restaurant/architecture.md`](../restaurant/architecture.md).
- **IMPLEMENTED** — Control-plane and entitlement evidence: [`docs/admin-platform/control-plane.md`](../admin-platform/control-plane.md), [`docs/admin-platform/entitlements.md`](../admin-platform/entitlements.md).

## Value hypotheses

- **PLANNED / RESEARCH INPUT (WHA-243 research task)** — Position around operational coherence, branch control, auditable exception handling, and fewer disconnected ledgers. Validate with restaurant owners, managers, cashiers, kitchen leads, and finance operators before using outcome claims.
- **PLANNED OWNER DECISION (WHA-243 owner decision)** — Choose primary ICP by restaurant format, branch count, current stack, and buying authority. No traction, savings, or productivity claim is repository-proved.

## Safe commercial language

Say “available in current repository” only for cited capabilities. Do not say deployed, certified, integrated with acquirers/cameras/printers, or proven to save time/money without separate acceptance evidence.
