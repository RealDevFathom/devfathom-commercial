# Go-to-market and onboarding

## Proposed motion

1. **PLANNED** — Qualify restaurant format, branches, workflows, integrations, security needs, hardware, and decision owner.
2. **PLANNED** — Demonstrate only repository-backed modules; record gaps and owners.
3. **IMPLEMENTED** — Seller control plane can record account/lead/opportunity/proposal/follow-up, convert a won opportunity to an organization record, configure package/contract/entitlements, and retain audit. Conversion does not auto-activate. Evidence: [`docs/admin-platform/commercial-ledger.md`](../admin-platform/commercial-ledger.md), [`src/app/platform/(console)/crm/page.tsx`](../../src/app/platform/%28console%29/crm/page.tsx).
4. **PLANNED OPERATING PROCESS** — Discovery, data/template preparation, branch/device/user setup, catalog configuration, role/SOP configuration, workflow rehearsal, acceptance sign-off, launch, and hypercare.
5. **PLANNED** — Expansion review based on validated adoption and operating outcomes, not unverified dashboard signals.

## Onboarding gates

- Named customer owner, agreed scope, package/contract, environment, identity/domain, data ownership, import validation, terminal/device inventory, training, rollback, support path, and signed acceptance.
- OCR, cameras, card-terminal integration, printing, shifts, and online gateways remain outside a standard promise until separately integrated and accepted. Evidence: [`docs/product/PRD.md`](../product/PRD.md).
