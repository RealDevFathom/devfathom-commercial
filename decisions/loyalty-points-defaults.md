# Loyalty points defaults

**Decision owner:** Saad  
**Status:** Binding

- Earn 1 point per complete Rs 100 (10,000 paisa) of eligible medicine/drug net paid.
- Earn 3 points per complete Rs 100 of eligible non-drug net paid.
- Floor each category subtotal independently.
- Value 1 point at Rs 1 (100 paisa) and require at least 200 points per redemption.
- Expire points six calendar months after earning and consume points FIFO.
- Keep the optional Gold tier disabled by default. When enabled, Rs 50,000 eligible spend in the
  rolling prior 12 months earns at 2x.
- Permit owners to configure rates, point value, redemption minimum, expiry and tier rules, with
  full old/new audit evidence.
